SPICY SWEET PAYROLL — DESKTOP APP
=================================

WINDOWS
  1. Unzip "SpicySweet-Payroll-Windows.zip" anywhere (e.g. your Desktop or C:\Apps).
  2. Open the folder and double-click "Spicy Sweet Payroll.exe".
     Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time:
     click "More info" → "Run anyway". (The app isn't code-signed.)
  3. Right-click the taskbar icon → "Pin to taskbar".

MAC
  1. Unzip "SpicySweet-Payroll-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip" (M1/M2/M3/M4 Macs)
     or "SpicySweet-Payroll-Mac-Intel.zip" (older Intel Macs).
  2. Drag "Spicy Sweet Payroll.app" into your Applications folder.
  3. First launch only — macOS blocks it because it isn't notarized:
     System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → "Open Anyway".
     (Or run once in Terminal:  xattr -cr "/Applications/Spicy Sweet Payroll.app")

SIGNING IN
  • The app opens the Spicy Sweet Payroll sign-in. Use your account
    (email + password). It stays signed in.
  • The owner approves every new account in Settings → People and picks a
    role: Owner, Co-owner, Manager, Head chatter or Chatter.
  • Everyone sees the same live data; chatters only see their own paystubs,
    stats card and the leaderboard.

iPHONE
  Open https://dalton007.github.io/spicysweet-payroll/ in Safari,
  tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". It opens full-screen with your logo.

MENU
  View → Back to Payroll (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+H) · Reload (Ctrl/Cmd+R)
  Help → Clear sign-in and app data on this computer
