01 · Accounts

Your whole team, signed in.

Chatters create their own account and wait for access to be granted to them by an Owner. Each chatter sees only their own shifts, paystubs, and analytic history; managers see everything.

  • Owner, manager and chatter roles
  • Synced through your cloud account, same data on every device
  • Rename a chatter and their roster slot updates with them

02 · Import

Drop in the Infloww report.

Two imports, auto-detected: the weekly chatter report and the biweekly creator statistics. Shifts, hours, sales and Messages Pro hours fill in by themselves.

  • Sales logged with 0 hours merge into the previous shift
  • Picked-up (double) shifts are recognised, not flagged as typos
  • Fuzzy name matching keeps everyone on the right roster slot

03 · Payroll

Payroll. One click.

Hourly pay, commission, goals and bonuses are calculated for every chatter the moment the report lands. Anything odd shows up in a short "things to check" list.

  • Tick items off one by one, or "Checked all & fixed all"
  • Shifts over 16.5h are errors; 8.5–16.5h are noted as picked-up shifts
  • Adjustments, strikes and custom pay per chatter

04 · Paystubs

Every dollar, explained.

Each chatter gets a paystub that shows exactly how their pay was built: hourly, commission tier, weekly goals, bonuses and adjustments with shift-by-shift detail.

  • Export as PDF or a shareable image
  • Exports keep the fixed brand look no matter which theme you use
  • Edit a line and the stub updates instantly

05 · Send out

Everyone paid, one card at a time.

The Send out deck lines up every paystub as a card. Open one, send it, press "Mark paid & next" it flips green and the next chatter slides in. The same deck exists for model invoices.

  • Progress bar and "Everyone's been paid" banner at the end
  • Copy a ready-made Discord message per chatter
  • Paid status syncs to the payroll table

  • Per chatter: revenue, targets hit and golden ratio, week by week
  • Agency-wide: revenue and payroll over the same weeks

10 · Model invoices

Invoices, drafted for you.

"Draft all" creates an invoice per model from the creator report, numbered in sequence and billed on net or gross. Edit lines, add expenses, export a clean PDF then send it from the Send out deck.

  • Agency details, bank info and numbering live in Settings
  • Clear drafts in one step when you want to start over
  • Invoice totals feed the Agency P&L

  • Works offline for the current page; syncs when back
